Three ways to study

📖 Practice Mode

Work through questions at your own pace with instant feedback and an explanation for every answer. Filter by category, search by keyword, and bookmark anything you want to revisit.

⏱️ Exam Simulator

Sixty questions against a two-hour countdown — the format of the actual UAG knowledge test — with results broken down by category and a one-click drill of the areas where you scored below 70%.

🃏 Flashcard Mode

Flip-card active recall for quick sessions. Mark each card “Know It” or “Need Review” and let the review modes resurface the questions you miss.
